              SEEK This workspace

                Each title is searched in each location. Empty means all of Australia.

                SEEK's robots.txt allows the first page of each search but not job pages or further result pages, so SEEK jobs are scored on the listing summary (about 20 newest-relevant results per title and location). SEEK may also refuse automated access altogether. Either way you can save SEEK search or job pages from your own browser (Ctrl+S) and import them here. A saved job page gives the full description. The browser extension (Settings) adds the jobs of a SEEK results page you have open in one go, also from SEEK's New to you view or a later page.

                Commute This workspace

                Company sources This workspace

                Careers pages or job boards. The app detects Workable, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters and Workday boards, otherwise it scrapes the page's HTML, respecting robots.txt and crawl delays.

                A careers page needs a browser component

                One of your careers pages shows its jobs only with JavaScript. To read it, JobHuntPA needs a browser component (Chromium, about 180 MB), downloaded once into your data folder. Nothing is downloaded unless you choose to.

                Settings

                Each card says what it applies to: this browser, this workspace, your account, or the whole of this JobHuntPA.

                Theme This browser

                Each card shows the app in that theme. Pick one with a click or tap, or with the arrow keys: it applies at once and is remembered in this browser. Every theme meets the WCAG AA contrast minimums, and labels or shapes always go with colours.

                Scheduled searches This workspace

                JobHuntPA runs this search by itself at set times while it is running; a search missed because it was closed runs if you start it within the hour. Each workspace has its own schedule, and each run scores the new jobs it finds, like Run search. Scheduled searches need background access (the card further down this tab); without it they are skipped. They are off in a new workspace: turn on Run searches automatically to start them.

                  On these days
                  Public holidays

                  The state picks the holiday calendar and also the time zone the times above are in. Substitute days, such as a weekend holiday moved to the Monday, are skipped too.

                  AI provider This JobHuntPA

                  The AI that scores jobs, writes cover letters, researches companies and answers job chats. Bring your own key: JobHuntPA sends your jobs and documents to this provider only when one of those features runs.

                  Provider, model and API key

                  Reasoning level

                  Higher levels think longer: usually better results, but slower and more expensive. The choices are the levels your model accepts; use Detect after changing model.

                  Fit scores This workspace

                  The buttons below rescore this workspace's jobs with the AI provider above.

                  AI usage This JobHuntPA

                  Requests and tokens sent to the AI provider, to compare with your provider's bill. Cached tokens are prompt tokens the provider served from its cache, usually at a discount.

                  Public address and host names This JobHuntPA

                  Only for using JobHuntPA from other devices, through an HTTPS proxy on this computer such as tailscale serve (the README's "Hosting on your own network" has the steps). Leave both empty when you only use it on this computer. Saving asks for your password if you entered it a while ago.

                  The https:// address other devices open. Sign-in cookies are then marked secure, and pages at that address are accepted. Changing it may sign this browser out.

                  Other names this computer is reached by, separated by commas (no http://, port or path). IP addresses always work.

                  Browser for JavaScript pages This JobHuntPA

                  A few careers pages show their jobs only with JavaScript. JobHuntPA reads those with a browser component (Chromium, through Playwright), about 180 MB, downloaded once from Playwright's servers into your data folder. Everything else works without it.

                  Server key This JobHuntPA

                  The server key protects the keys JobHuntPA keeps for background searches. Replacing it moves all of them to a new key and drops the old one. Do it if the data folder or a backup of it may have been copied by someone else. It asks for your password and takes a few seconds.
