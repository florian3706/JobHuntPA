    Trouble signing in?

    • Remembered your password? Sign in instead.
    • Use the name you sign in with (on an install from before names, the email address it was made with). Capitals and spaces around it don't matter.
    • Each recovery code works once. After 5 wrong tries in a row, JobHuntPA asks you to wait a little before the next one.
    • No recovery code left? Without your password or a recovery code, your encrypted data can't be read by anyone. The reset tool on the computer JobHuntPA runs on starts over: it deletes the account and its data and keeps the settings (see the README).
