            SEEK

            Search SEEK
              Each title is searched in each location. Empty = all of Australia.

              SEEK's robots.txt allows the first page of each search but not job pages or further result pages, so SEEK jobs are scored on the listing summary (about 20 newest-relevant results per title and location). SEEK may also refuse automated access altogether. Either way you can save SEEK search or job pages from your own browser (Ctrl+S) and import them here. A saved job page gives the full description.

              Commute

              Used on each job's full page (Open ↗): public transport and driving times to the office at peak times, there and back.

                Stations you'd walk or drive to; each gets its own journey. Empty = from your home pin. Driving always starts at the home pin.

                Company sources

                Careers pages or job boards. The app detects Workable/Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/SmartRecruiters/Workday boards, otherwise it scrapes the page's HTML, respecting robots.txt and crawl delays.

                Documents

                PDF / DOCX / ODT / TXT, or notes you write here

                Write a note

                Plain text the AI reads like an uploaded document: details of a project, results you're proud of, or what you want from your next job (choose Preferences for that).

                The scorer reads resumes first, then cover letters, then other documents and notes, and treats everything but resumes as supporting evidence only. Preferences say what you want from a job: the scorer names jobs that conflict with them but never counts them as experience, the chat and title suggestions use them, and cover letters never see them. Untick "use for scoring" to keep a document out of every prompt. Changing documents marks existing scores as stale.

                One summary instead of many documents

                Every ticked document goes with every request to the AI, repeats and all. Create summary merges the ticked documents (not preferences) into one new document: each fact once, with every employer, date, number and qualification kept. It takes a few minutes. Read it through, edit it if needed, then untick the originals so they aren't sent twice. Each document can also get a shorter concise version of its own: Open it below.

                Map

                The jobs your Jobs tab filters show, with your pins and their radii. Pan and zoom: the jobs in view are listed under the map. Click a dot, or Show in the list, to see its jobs; Open ↗ opens a job's full page in a new tab. With the map focused, the arrow keys pan and + and − zoom.

                Click or tap the map to drop a pin, or move the map until the cross in its middle is on the place and press Drop a pin at the cross. Drag a pin to move it, or use Move to the cross in the list of pins. With the map focused, the arrow keys pan and + and − zoom. Home pin: hybrid and onsite roles within its radius are OK. Hybrid pins (e.g. stations on your commute): hybrid roles within the radius are OK. Onsite pins: onsite roles within the radius are OK. Remote roles don't need a pin.

                Settings

                Each card says whether it applies to this workspace or to all of them

                Theme This browser

                Each card shows the app in that theme. Pick one with a click or tap, or with the arrow keys: it applies at once and is remembered in this browser. Every theme meets the WCAG AA contrast minimums, and labels or shapes always go with colours.

                Scheduled searches This workspace

                JobHuntPA runs this search by itself at set times while it is running; a search missed because it was closed runs if you start it within the hour. Each workspace has its own schedule, and each run scores the new jobs it finds, like Run search. Scheduled searches need background access (the card further down this tab); without it they are skipped.
