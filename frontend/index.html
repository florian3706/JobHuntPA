              SEEK

              Search SEEK
                Each title is searched in each location. Empty = all of Australia.

                SEEK's robots.txt allows the first page of each search but not job pages or further result pages, so SEEK jobs are scored on the listing summary (about 20 newest-relevant results per title and location). SEEK may also refuse automated access altogether. Either way you can save SEEK search or job pages from your own browser (Ctrl+S) and import them here. A saved job page gives the full description. The browser extension (Settings) adds the jobs of a SEEK results page you have open in one go, also from SEEK's New to you view or a later page.

                Commute

                  Company sources

                  Careers pages or job boards. The app detects Workable/Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby/SmartRecruiters/Workday boards, otherwise it scrapes the page's HTML, respecting robots.txt and crawl delays.

                  A careers page needs a browser component

                  One of your careers pages shows its jobs only with JavaScript. To read it, JobHuntPA needs a browser component (Chromium, about 180 MB), downloaded once into your data folder. Nothing is downloaded unless you choose to.

                  Documents

                  PDF / DOCX / ODT / TXT, or notes you write here

                  Write a note

                  Plain text the AI reads like an uploaded document: details of a project, results you're proud of, or what you want from your next job (choose Preferences for that).

                  The scorer reads resumes first, then cover letters, then other documents and notes, and treats everything but resumes as supporting evidence only. Preferences say what you want from a job: the scorer names jobs that conflict with them but never counts them as experience, the chat and title suggestions use them, and cover letters never see them. Untick "use for scoring" to keep a document out of every prompt. Changing documents marks existing scores as stale.

                  One summary instead of many documents

                  Every ticked document goes with every request to the AI, repeats and all. Create summary merges the ticked documents (not preferences) into one new document: each fact once, with every employer, date, number and qualification kept. It takes a few minutes. Read it through, edit it if needed, then untick the originals so they aren't sent twice. Each document can also get a shorter concise version of its own: Open it below.

                  Suggested job titles

                  Reads this workspace's documents and suggests job titles to search for. Tick the ones you want, then add them to this search or start a new search with them.

                  Map

                  The jobs your Jobs tab filters show, with your pins and their radii. Pan and zoom: the jobs in view are listed under the map. Click a dot, or Show in the list, to see its jobs; Open ↗ opens a job's full page in a new tab. With the map focused, the arrow keys pan and + and − zoom.

                  Click or tap the map to drop a pin, or move the map until the cross in its middle is on the place and press Drop a pin at the cross. Drag a pin to move it, or use Move to the cross in the list of pins. With the map focused, the arrow keys pan and + and − zoom. Home pin: hybrid and onsite roles within its radius are OK. Hybrid pins (e.g. stations on your commute): hybrid roles within the radius are OK. Onsite pins: onsite roles within the radius are OK. Remote roles don't need a pin.

                  Settings

                  Each card says what it applies to: this browser, this workspace, your account, or the whole of this JobHuntPA

                  Theme This browser

                  Each card shows the app in that theme. Pick one with a click or tap, or with the arrow keys: it applies at once and is remembered in this browser. Every theme meets the WCAG AA contrast minimums, and labels or shapes always go with colours.

                  Scheduled searches This workspace

                  JobHuntPA runs this search by itself at set times while it is running; a search missed because it was closed runs if you start it within the hour. Each workspace has its own schedule, and each run scores the new jobs it finds, like Run search. Scheduled searches need background access (the card further down this tab); without it they are skipped. They are off in a new workspace: tick Run searches automatically to start them.

                    AI provider This JobHuntPA

                    The AI that scores jobs, writes cover letters, researches companies and answers job chats. Bring your own key: JobHuntPA sends your jobs and documents to this provider only when one of those features runs.

                    Reasoning level

                    Higher levels think longer: usually better results, but slower and more expensive. The choices are the levels your model accepts; use Detect after changing model.

                    AI usage This JobHuntPA

                    Requests and tokens sent to the AI provider, to compare with your provider's bill. Cached tokens are prompt tokens the provider served from its cache, usually at a discount.

                    Browser for JavaScript pages This JobHuntPA

                    A few careers pages show their jobs only with JavaScript. JobHuntPA reads those with a browser component (Chromium, through Playwright), about 180 MB, downloaded once from Playwright's servers into your data folder. Everything else works without it.

                    Server key This JobHuntPA

                    The server key protects the keys JobHuntPA keeps for background searches. Replacing it moves all of them to a new key and drops the old one. Do it if the data folder or a backup of it may have been copied by someone else. It asks for your password and takes a few seconds.
