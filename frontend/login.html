    On this device, until you sign out or haven't used JobHuntPA for 30 days. Don't tick it on a shared computer.

    Trouble signing in?

    • Use the name you chose for your account (on an install from before names, the email address it was made with). Capitals and spaces around it don't matter; in the password they do.
    • Your password manager can fill in both fields, and pasting works too.
    • After 5 wrong passwords in a row, JobHuntPA asks you to wait a little before the next try.
    • Forgotten your password? I have a recovery code: it lets you set a new password and keep your data.
    • No password and no recovery code? The reset tool on the computer JobHuntPA runs on starts over: it deletes the account and its data and keeps the settings (see the README).
